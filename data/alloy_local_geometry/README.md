# Single-site substitution: local chemistry versus network relaxation

One isovalent substitution per cell in the fixed 0-GPa parent lattice (ISIF = 2, B-site fraction 1/16, lowest-index site of the element): Mg(Sc<sub>0.25</sub>Sb<sub>0.75</sub>)<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> Sb→Sc and Sc→Sb (`k12_sb2_sc`, `k12_sc2_sb`, 56 atoms), MgSc<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> Sc→Sb (56-atom conventional cell) and CaTiO<sub>3</sub> Ti→Sn (80-atom 2×1×2 Pnma supercell). PBE, 520 eV; relaxations to 10<sup>−3</sup> eV/Å; ISYM = 0 statics on Γ-centred meshes containing the eight TRIM points (4×4×4 spinels, 2×4×2 CaTiO<sub>3</sub>).

States: s0 (parent), s1 (substituted, parent geometry), relaxed structure, and four states built from the network displacement u (s1 → relaxed, centroid removed), split into a bond-preserving part u<sub>bp</sub> (kernel of the first-order B–X bond Jacobian, six nearest X per B at s1) and a bond-changing part u<sub>bc</sub>: 00 (network at parent positions, A cations relaxed), 10 (+u<sub>bp</sub>), 01 (+u<sub>bc</sub>), 11 (relaxed).
Margins Λ<sub>V</sub>, Λ<sub>C</sub>: energy difference between the edge TRIM point and the second-best TRIM point, both chosen at state 11. Decomposition: Δ<sub>chem</sub> = Λ(s1) − Λ(s0), Δ<sub>A</sub> = Λ(00) − Λ(s1), Δ<sub>bc</sub> = Λ(01) − Λ(00), Δ<sub>bp</sub> = Λ(10) − Λ(00); R<sub>rel</sub> = |Δ<sub>bc</sub> + Δ<sub>bp</sub>| / (|Δ<sub>chem</sub>| + |Δ<sub>bc</sub> + Δ<sub>bp</sub>|), φ<sub>sub</sub> = |Δ<sub>bp</sub>| / (|Δ<sub>bp</sub>| + |Δ<sub>bc</sub>|).
In MgSc<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> Sc→Sb the two highest valence TRIM points are symmetry-degenerate (Λ<sub>V</sub> ≡ 0), and the VBM moves from Γ to (¼, −¼, ¼) already at s1.

- `results/<case>/geometry.json`: bond-preserving shares of u and of the drive (s1 forces, first ionic step), their ratio, displacement amplitudes; `readout.json`: margins of all states, decomposition, R<sub>rel</sub>, φ<sub>sub</sub>, edge k points, energies; `meta.json`: case definition.
- Scripts: `alloy_prepare.py` (s0, s1 and relaxation inputs; parent structures in `inputs/`, POTCAR library of the cluster not deposited), `alloy_run.sh` (job sequence), `alloy_decomposition.py` (displacement split, four-state inputs, `geometry.json`), `alloy_readout.py` (`readout.json`).
