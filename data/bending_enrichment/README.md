# Bond-preserving enrichment

Bond-preserving shares of the compression drive, of the force-balance prediction and of the actual relaxation for every structure with a finite-difference force-constant matrix: Mg(Sc<sub>1−x</sub>Sb<sub>x</sub>)<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> x = 0.75 (k12, tightly relaxed, 4→5 GPa) and x = 0.5 (k08, 0→5 GPa), CaTiO<sub>3</sub>, CaSnO<sub>3</sub>, rock-salt-ordered Ca<sub>2</sub>SnTiO<sub>6</sub> and Ca<sub>8</sub>Sn<sub>6</sub>Ti<sub>2</sub>O<sub>24</sub> (0→5 GPa). Existing force constants only; no new DFT.

- Bond-preserving subspace: kernel of the first-order B–X bond-length Jacobian, bond vectors from the start fractional coordinates in the end lattice (r<sub>aff</sub>); network displacements are centred. share(x) = |Px|²/|x|² over network atoms. Drive f = DFT force at r<sub>aff</sub>; prediction H⁻¹f; actual response u = relaxation displacement; enrichment = response share / drive share.
- Two constructions of H with the same Jacobian and share function: full 3N×3N central differences (±0.01 Å, translations removed) for k12, CaTiO<sub>3</sub> and CaSnO<sub>3</sub>; symmetric-sector H<sub>s</sub> = −Sᵀ∂F/∂S (S an orthonormal basis of space-group-preserving displacements) for k08, Ca<sub>2</sub>SnTiO<sub>6</sub> and Ca<sub>8</sub>Sn<sub>6</sub>Ti<sub>2</sub>O<sub>24</sub>. The sector is exact here: the 0→5 GPa paths keep the space group (drive symmetry residual 1e−5 to 2e−4) and both u and H⁻¹f lie in the sector.
- `enrichment_all_systems.csv` / `.json` (from `enrichment_all.py`): drive, predicted and actual shares, both enrichments, cos(H⁻¹f, u), atom, network-atom, bond and bond-preserving-dof counts; for sector structures also the sector dimension, drive symmetry residual and number of negative eigenvalues.
- Inputs: full H of `force_balance_reference`, tight drive and endpoints of `spinel_pressure_path/force_balance`, `full_force_balance/{ca_tio3,ca_sno3}/p0_p5/` (`meta.json`, H as `H.npy`, per-displacement forces `hessian/*/forces.npy` with `result.json`), and `sector_force_balance/*/p0_p5/` from `sector_force_balance_prepare.py` and `sector_force_balance_analyze.py` (`meta.json`, `force_balance_sym.json`, sector basis `S.npy`, H<sub>s</sub> as `Hs.npy`, per-displacement `hessian/*/forces.npy` and `result.json` with energy and maximum force).
