# Four-state leverage

Property–energy leverage of the bending and complement channels for eight compressions: Mg(Sc<sub>1−x</sub>Sb<sub>x</sub>)<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> x = 0.75 tightly relaxed at 4→5 GPa (`k12_t45`) and 0→5 GPa (`k12_05`), x = 0.5 (`k08`), layered NaScInO<sub>2</sub>, CaTiO<sub>3</sub> and CaSnO<sub>3</sub> (group A), rock-salt Ca<sub>2</sub>SnTiO<sub>6</sub> and Ca<sub>8</sub>Sn<sub>6</sub>Ti<sub>2</sub>O<sub>24</sub> (group B), all 0→5 GPa unless stated.

- The force-balance response u<sub>pred</sub> = H⁻¹f is split into u<sub>b</sub> = Pu<sub>pred</sub> (bond-preserving part of the centred network displacement, zero on non-network atoms) and u<sub>c</sub> = u<sub>pred</sub> − u<sub>b</sub>; E<sub>b</sub> + E<sub>c</sub> = E<sub>total</sub> to 1e−14 meV. Margin gradients g<sub>i</sub> = [Λ(+) − Λ(−)]/(2·0.25|u<sub>i</sub>|) come from statics at r<sub>aff</sub> ± 0.25|u<sub>i</sub>|d<sub>i</sub>; φ<sub>pred</sub> = g<sub>b</sub>|u<sub>b</sub>|/(g<sub>b</sub>|u<sub>b</sub>| + g<sub>c</sub>|u<sub>c</sub>|) is compared with the four-state φ. INCAR, KPOINTS and POTCAR are those of each structure's four-state state00 (VASP 6.5.1).
- Margins: the published four-state margins for group A (CaTiO<sub>3</sub> Γ–U and CaSnO<sub>3</sub> U–Z valence valleys); for group B the band edge with the smaller |Λ| between the best and second-best TRIM on the relaxed 5-GPa state11 (`groupb_margin.json`: valence edge for both, Λ<sub>V</sub> = 25.9 and 72.1 meV). The u<sub>c</sub> channel contains the A-cation motion that the four-state construction places in state00; Λ(state00) − Λ(r<sub>aff</sub>) is reported as `diag_Lambda_state00_minus_raff_meV` and resolved in `three_channel`.
- `prepare_report.json` (`scripts/lev_prepare.py`): reproduced enrichment shares, norms of u<sub>b</sub>, u<sub>c</sub> and their A-cation and network parts, harmonic energies. `evaluation.json` (`scripts/lev_evaluate.py`): measured and predicted ΔΛ<sub>b</sub>, ΔΛ<sub>c</sub>, φ, energy shares, and the agreement flags C1–C4 defined in that script. `calculations/<structure>/leverage.json` (`scripts/lev_readout.py`): Λ, energies and gradients of each static, from the drive at r<sub>aff</sub> and the split response in the same folder (`F_raff.npy`, `u_pred.npy`, `u_b.npy`, `u_c.npy`); the statics themselves (r<sub>aff</sub> `raff`, finite-difference points `bp`, `bm`, `cp`, `cm`, and for group B the four states in `fourstate/`) with INCAR, KPOINTS, POSCAR, CONTCAR and OSZICAR in the same folder.
- Other scripts: `lev_common.py` (shared definitions), `lev_build_group_b.py` (records the group-B φ<sub>pred</sub> before the group-B four states are built, then builds them), `lev_four_state_build.py`, `lev_run_scf.sh` (one VASP static). `group_a_fourstate/<structure>/` holds the group-A four-state inputs (state00–state11) and their `phi.json` read-outs.
