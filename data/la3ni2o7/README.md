# La3Ni2O7 under pressure and biaxial strain

Non-magnetic PBE (and PBE+U, U = 4 eV on Ni d) calculations on the 48-atom Amam cell of La3Ni2O7 (VASP, PAW La/Ni_pv/O, ENCUT 520 eV).

- Structures: `poscar_exp` (Ling et al. 2000, `make_poscar.py`), tilt-free reference `poscar_fmmm_start` (`make_fmmm.py`); relaxed 0/4/8 GPa and −1/−2% strained structures with their INCARs in `relaxation/` (`run_relaxation.sh`, `run_direct.sh`, `run_eta2_multi.sh`, `run_fmmm_cpu.sh`); comparison with experiment in `relaxation_vs_experiment.md`.
- Force balance on the intervals P1 (0→4 GPa), P2 (4→8 GPa), S1 (0→−1%), S2 (−1→−2%): `force_balance/` (finite-displacement inputs in `calculations/<interval>/<case>/`, `modes.json`, forces in `forces/`, `force_balance_result.json`, `force_balance_result_reference_only.json`, `newton_step_result.json`), from `force_balance_prepare.py`, `force_balance_job.sh`, `force_balance_analyze.py`, `force_balance_geometric_factor.py`, `newton_step_prepare.py`, `newton_step_analyze.py`; summary in `force_balance_result.md`.
- Four-state readouts R = (top of the interlayer-bonding d_z² band at Γ) − E_F: `four_state/fourstate.json`, `four_state/readout.json` (inputs in `four_state/pbe/`, `pbe_u4/` (U = 4 eV) and `pbe_k19/` (19×19×5 mesh), keyed `runs/`, `runs_U/` and `runs_k19/` in `readout.json`), from `four_state_build.py`, `four_state_prepare.py`, `four_state_job.sh`, `four_state_readout.py`; summary in `four_state_result.md`.
- Wannier decomposition, affine step and interlayer coupling t⊥: `wannier/` (Wannier inputs in `calculations/<interval>/<state>/`, 0-GPa readout inputs in `readout/`, `affine.json`, `wannier*_<interval>.json`, `wannier_table.md`, `print_wannier_classes.py`), from `wannier_prepare.py`, `wannier_job.sh`, `wannier_job_cpu.sh`, `wannier_decomposition.py`, `wannier_decomposition_fixed_n.py`, `affine_step.py`, `wannier_table.py`, `interlayer_hopping.py`; summary in `wannier_result.md`.
- Octahedral tilt at the −2% and experimental film lattices: `film_tilt/` (`run_film_tilt.sh`, `run_tilt_readout.sh`, `tilt_readout.py`).
- Drive-plane grid in (a_p, c) with affine/internal split of ΔR and Δt⊥: relaxed and affine structures, `attribution.txt` and `tperp_aff.txt` in `drive_plane_grid/` (`run_grid.sh`).
- γ-pair and partner charge at Γ on the bend plane (Fig. 4a shading): `orbital/` (README, `orbital_slices.py`, `slices.npz`, `bands.json`).
- `fig_la327_unified_data.csv`: data of the La3Ni2O7 figure panels (a geometry, c ΔR, d Δt⊥, e t⊥ against straightening); bilayer projections from `bilayer_geom.py`. Shared helpers: `la327_network.py` (Ni–O bond network and bond-preserving projector).
