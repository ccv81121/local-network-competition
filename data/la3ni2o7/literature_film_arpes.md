# Published photoemission on La3Ni2O7-family films (literature values used in SI Note 6)

Checked against the published PRX text by the author on 2026-10-31.

- Wang et al., Phys. Rev. X 16, 031008 (2026) (rWang26):
  - top of the gamma band, tight-binding fit (Table II): bulk -50 meV; undoped (La,Pr)3Ni2O7 film -57 meV;
  - the gamma top rises from about -85 meV (thinnest film) to about -45 meV with thickness (Fig. 4c);
  - DFT+U puts the gamma band deeper under compressive strain, up to about -350 meV at 2.5%, and the ARPES confirms this qualitatively;
  - undoped films keep gamma below E_F; Ca doping brings it to or through E_F, and this is not tied to superconductivity;
  - gamma-band renormalization 7.3 +- 1.9 (bulk 5.5).
- Li et al., Natl. Sci. Rev. 12, nwaf205 (2025) (rLiChen25): superconducting (La,Pr)3Ni2O7/SrLaAlO4 films show hole doping relative to bulk, with d_z2 weight at E_F (abstract).
- Computed shift of E_gamma on the film lattice with U = 4 eV: -321 meV in total, of which the affine step gives -248 meV (drive_plane_grid/attribution.txt, row film).
