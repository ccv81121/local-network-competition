# Rendered CBM wavefunctions

VESTA renderings of Re ψ of the conduction-band minimum of Mg(Sc1−xSbx)2Se4 (x = 0.75) at 5 GPa (four-state state 11), centred on the atom of largest weight in each state; used as the wavefunction panels of Fig. 3.

- `cb_gamma_sc24.png`: Γ valley, centred on Sc24 (Sc d character).
- `cb_r_sb20.png`: R valley, centred on Sb20 (Sb p–Se s character).
- Cube and VESTA inputs are written by `spinel_fatbands/wavefunction_cell.py` from the WAVECAR of the 5-GPa run.
