# Colocation relations for spinels

Single-coordinate and dual-edge relations that classify whether the valence and conduction edges of substituted AB2X4 spinels sit at Γ, evaluated with fixed formulas on 681 states (440 development, 224 challenge, 17 excluded); edge labels are primitive-cell k-point identities.

- `colocation_relation/best_performance_formula.json`: the single coordinate Π_S (19 descriptors, 40 operator nodes; orientation −1, threshold −114.17). Ratios use a/(|b| + ε) with ε = 10⁻⁶ × the 75th percentile of the scaled |b| (681 states for Π_S; 440 development states for the dual-edge coordinates Π_V, Π_C).
- Descriptor scales: `shared_19_primitive_scales.csv` (shared component) and `dual_12_primitive_scales.csv` (development IQR, else 1.4826 × MAD, else standard deviation, else 1); thresholds τ_V = −9.038, τ_C = 42.36 in `readout_thresholds.json`; decision state of Π_S (descriptor names, thresholds and orientations of the top 30 relations) in `pi_s_decision_state.json`, its ratio convention in `pi_s_ratio_semantics.json` and its metrics on the 442 development and 239 challenge states in `pi_s_metrics.json`.
- `compute_relation_scores.py` → `relation_scores_681.csv`, `relation_parameters.json` (offsets, IQRs, recomputation errors, 442-state metrics), checked against the archived scores `reference_relation_scores.csv`; it reads float64 arrays of the source database that are not included here (retained columns in `retained_feature_indices.json`, descriptor names in `formula_full_feature_names.json`, development states in `dual_dev_sample_ids.csv`). `dual_edge_validation.py` is the evaluator; its outputs are `edge_metrics_by_partition.csv`, `run_summary.json`, `validation_protocol.json` and the Π_S and threshold records above.
- `state_selection.py` → `state_selection.txt`: which of the 1003 calculated states the relations use. 321 are metallic and excluded. The relations use the 442 nonmetallic states of 32 parent compounds. The other 240 nonmetallic states belong to ten further parent compounds, none shared with the 32: their element distribution differs from the main set (In, Ir, Hf, Ta or Ru, absent from the 442 states, occur in 213 of them), and more of them have a gap that vanishes within the energy resolution of the calculations (88 of 240, against 39 of 442).
- `spinel_sc_sb_ordering_margins.csv`: Λ_V, Λ_C and edge k points of the Sc/Sb orderings along x in Mg(Sc1−xSbx)2Se4; `spinel_sc_sb_structure_source_rows.csv`: their source records.
- `sr_singlecoord_feature_ablation_mapped.csv`: BACC change when each descriptor of the 30 parent relations is removed, with its channel (local geometry, network propagation, ...).
