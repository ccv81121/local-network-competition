# Three-channel decomposition

Decomposition of the margin increment of each four-state construction (`four_state_leverage`) into bending b, the network complement n and the A-site channel A. Structures: Mg(Sc<sub>1−x</sub>Sb<sub>x</sub>)<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>4</sub> x = 0.75 at 4→5 GPa (`k12_t45`) and 0→5 GPa (`k12_05`) in `tc_result.json`; x = 0.5 (`k08`), CaTiO<sub>3</sub>, CaSnO<sub>3</sub>, layered NaScInO<sub>2</sub>, Ca<sub>2</sub>SnTiO<sub>6</sub> and Ca<sub>8</sub>Sn<sub>6</sub>Ti<sub>2</sub>O<sub>24</sub> in `all_network/tca_result.json`.

- Predicted: ΔΛ<sub>b</sub> = g<sub>b</sub>|u<sub>b</sub>| and ΔΛ<sub>c</sub> = g<sub>c</sub>|u<sub>c</sub>| from the four-state gradients, ΔΛ<sub>A</sub> = g<sub>A</sub>|u<sub>A</sub>| with g<sub>A</sub> from two statics at r<sub>aff</sub> ± 0.25|u<sub>A</sub>| along the A-site part u<sub>A</sub> of u<sub>c</sub>, and ΔΛ<sub>n</sub> = ΔΛ<sub>c</sub> − ΔΛ<sub>A</sub>. Measured: b and n from the four states, A = Λ(state00) − Λ(r<sub>aff</sub>).
- Result keys: `pred_meV`, `meas_meV`, shares, totals, and the agreement flags T0 (signs of all three channels agree), T1 (every share within 0.10), T2 (A share within 0.10 with the same sign), T3 (totals within 10%); T1–T3 are evaluated where |ΔΛ<sub>total</sub>| ≥ 3 meV.
- Scripts: `three_channel_prepare.py` (A-channel POSCARs), `three_channel_readout.py` (margins → `tc_readout.json`), `three_channel_evaluate.py` (→ `tc_result.json`); `all_network/three_channel_all_network.py prepare|eval` for the other six structures. Margins use the Λ rule of `four_state_leverage/scripts/lev_common.py`. `a_channel_steps.json` (in both folders) records per structure the A-site atoms, |u<sub>A</sub>| and the step length 0.25|u<sub>A</sub>| of the two A-channel statics, with checksums of their POSCARs; it is read by `three_channel_evaluate.py` and `three_channel_all_network.py eval`.
