# Validation of the 24 Wannier Hamiltonians

`wannier_24_validation.csv`: the task-audit rows of `source_support/hamiltonian_mechanisms.csv` in the archive ($ARCHIVE/01_evidence/01_core_freeze/04_MICROSCOPIC_HAMILTONIAN), one row per Hamiltonian. All 24 pass the independent validation and reproduce the DFT ordering of the compared extrema. Maximum errors after the common energy alignment: VBM 1.065 meV, CBM 1.947 meV, gap 2.180 meV (SI Note 6; main-text Methods "within 2.2 meV"). The ten Hamiltonians of Fig. 4a are among these 24.
